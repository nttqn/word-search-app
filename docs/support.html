Support for WordHunt - Tra Từ

Last updated: September 17, 2026

WordHunt - Tra Từ is a word-search puzzle game for Android and iOS built as an English vocabulary trainer: find English words hidden in the letter grid, and each one reveals its Vietnamese meaning right in the clue list.

How to play

  • Drag across the grid in a straight line (horizontal, vertical, or diagonal) to select a word from the clue list — you can drag from either end.
  • Finding a word crosses it off the list and reveals its Vietnamese meaning underneath.
  • Find all the words to complete the puzzle — score and time are tracked, and your best score per level is saved locally.
  • Use the hint button (3 per puzzle) if you're stuck — it briefly reveals one unfound word.
  • Four difficulty levels (Cơ bản / Trung cấp / Nâng cao / Chuyên sâu), each with its own word bank and grid size — replaying a level gives you a fresh set of words.

Common questions

The leaderboard says "not available"

The leaderboard uses Google Play Games Services (Android) or Game Center (iOS). Make sure you're signed in to a Google account (Android) or Game Center (iOS) on your device, and that you have an internet connection.

I don't hear any sound

Check the sound toggle in the top-right corner on the home screen or in-game, and make sure your device isn't in silent/mute mode.

Something's broken, or you have feedback

Please include your device model and OS version (e.g. "Pixel 8, Android 15" or "iPhone 15, iOS 18") along with a description of what happened — it helps a lot in tracking issues down.
